The rationale explains what is worth learning, followed by the reported evidence and its limits. P&G and BCG are research references for evaluating concept work. ## Leading in the age of AI

Where should designers, engineers, and specialists work together when AI changes who can do the work?

**[Adyen — reviewing real components while a UI is being built](https://www.figma.com/customers/how-adyen-is-rebuilding-design/)**

- **Why it belongs.** It gives design leaders a concrete model for moving critique into implementation. Designers and engineers reviewed generated interfaces together against production components and tokens. Adyen reports selected UI builds fell from 2–3 engineering days to about 2 hours of joint work. Hong Zou and Hanbin Chang explain the collaboration in their [workflow presentation](https://www.figma.com/webinars/workflow-lab-adyen-mcp/).

**[trivago — connecting UX writing and localization](https://tech.trivago.com/post/2026-10-02-from-tools-to-teammates-how-ai-empowered-our-ux-team-to-level-up)**

- **Why it belongs.** Useful for design leaders rebuilding UX-content operations around AI, review, and localization. Ankia Wolf, Sami Hammami, Dan Ardeleanu, and colleagues linked drafting, translation keys, and translation requests, with checks for terminology, placeholders, and character limits. The team reports 75% shorter writing turnaround and 41% more output. It says quality stayed high, with no published scoring rubric.

## Defining and measuring impact

What would convince you that a faster workflow produced better work? These teams measured elapsed time alongside a separate quality or performance outcome.

**[Procter & Gamble — measuring the quality of early product ideas](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w33641/w33641.pdf)**

- **Why it belongs.** Its experiment gives design leaders a reference for evaluating AI-assisted concept work. In a randomized workshop with 776 professionals, AI-assisted individuals took 16.4% less time and earned blind expert scores 0.37 standard deviations higher than unaided individuals. Borrow the combination of a comparison group, elapsed time, and independent quality assessment. Findings cover proposals before commercialization.

**[Intuit TurboTax — testing campaign creative against a control](https://business.adobe.com/blog/real-time-insights-and-on-demand-creative-for-turbotax)**

- **Why it belongs.** It connects AI-assisted creative production to audience response, giving design leaders a basis for scaling a workflow. After a first pilot missed its target, a second used Adobe GenStudio for ad assembly, copy, and refreshes. Adobe reports turnaround falling from up to 2 weeks to as little as 4 hours, with 38% higher click-through than control ads.

## Craft and quality

Which parts of the work deserve an expert review, and which can the team protect through its production process?

**[BCG — judging an idea separately from its presentation](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838)**

- **Why it belongs.** Its experiment helps design leaders define quality criteria for AI-assisted concept development. On a footwear brief, GPT-4 users without additional training reached the final question 27.6% faster and earned 29.9% higher overall grades. Specialists scored idea creativity 11.7% higher. Use the difference to evaluate originality separately from the overall quality of a proposal.

**[Unilever — preserving packaging fidelity in generated campaign images](https://www.unilever.com/news/press-and-media/press-releases/2025/unilever-reinvents-product-shoots-with-digital-twins-and-ai/)**

- **Why it belongs.** It offers design leaders a concrete mechanism for protecting brand craft as AI expands creative production. Teams retained approved product packaging in reusable 3D models while generating surrounding scenes. For TRESemmé Thailand, Unilever reports 2× production speed, 87% lower content costs, and a 5% increase in purchase intent. The commercial measure was stated intent.

## Customer value and trust

What can a customer do differently, and what evidence shows the change mattered?

**[Mitsubishi Electric — letting clients change a prototype during the meeting](https://www.figma.com/customers/mitsubishi-electric-corporation/)**

- **Why it belongs.** It shows how design leaders can use AI prototyping to create more opportunities for client feedback. Working with Serendie components and Figma Make, one app team reduced prototyping from 2 weeks to 1 day, increased demos from 3 to 12, and shortened validation from 10 weeks to 6. The evidence covers feedback opportunities and validation time.

**[Microsoft Copilot — asking customers to judge responses to their own prompts](https://outset.ai/resources/stories/how-microsoft-increased-copilot-retention-by-five-percent-with-outset)**

- **Why it belongs.** It gives leaders a way to ground AI evaluation in customers’ tasks. Christopher Monnier, Chuck Kwong, and Wendy Wang’s [research method](https://microsoft.design/articles/putting-the-user-back-into-ai-evaluation/) connects explanations of response quality to design decisions. The wider program combined human interviews with AI moderation. Microsoft internally estimated roughly 5% higher retention after changes, with no published comparison group or retention window.

**[Duolingo — widening access to listening practice](https://blog.duolingo.com/scaling-duoradio/)**

- **Why it belongs.** It shows how learning designers can translate AI production capacity into a broader customer offering. They grounded scripts in curricula and checked language level and coherence. DuoRadio expanded from 300 to 15,000+ episodes in fewer than two quarters, while daily sessions grew from 500,000 to 5 million. Study the combination of expert standards and scaled access. Learning gains require separate evaluation.
